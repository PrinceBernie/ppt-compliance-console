## 🎨 **Design Principles Applied**

### **1. Consistency**
✅ Uniform border radius (12px standard, 16px for large cards)
✅ Consistent spacing (1rem, 1.5rem, 2rem)
✅ Standardized shadows and gradients

### **2. Hierarchy**
✅ Clear visual weight (headers → subheaders → body)
✅ Color-coded importance (red = primary, grey = secondary)
✅ Size differentiation (2rem metrics, 1.1rem subheaders)

### **3. Accessibility**
✅ High contrast text (white on red, dark on light)
✅ Clear focus states
✅ Readable font sizes (minimum 0.875rem)

### **4. Modern Aesthetics**
✅ Gradients for depth
✅ Shadows for elevation
✅ Rounded corners for friendliness
✅ Smooth transitions for polish

### **5. Brand Alignment**
✅ Red as primary color throughout
✅ Grey for supporting elements
✅ White for clarity and space
✅ Consistent brand presence
